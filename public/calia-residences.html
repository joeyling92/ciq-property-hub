07 — FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Project & Developer

What is Calia Residences?

Calia Residences is a freehold serviced apartment development by PGB Desa Heights Sdn Bhd (subsidiary of Paragon Globe Berhad), located along the Danga Bay shoreline in Johor Bahru. The name is derived from the Greek word "Kallos," meaning beauty. The development comprises 2 towers with 1,274 units across 4 unit types (453–984 sqft), 28 holistic lifestyle facilities on Level 7 & 7A, GreenRE Gold certification, and a complimentary shuttle service to CIQ and the Bukit Chagar RTS station.

Who is the developer of Calia Residences?

Calia Residences is developed by PGB Desa Heights Sdn Bhd, a subsidiary of Paragon Globe Berhad — a public company listed on the Main Market of Bursa Malaysia. PGB is also the developer behind The Iconic by PGB in Stulang Darat, Johor Bahru. Developer contact: Level 10-01, Grand Paragon Hotel, No. 18, Jalan Harimau, Taman Century, 80250 Johor Bahru · +607-278 6668 · marketing@pgbgroup.com.my · Mon–Sat 10am–6pm, Sun & PH 10am–4pm.

Where exactly is Calia Residences located?

Calia Residences is situated along the Danga Bay shoreline in Johor Bahru. The site covers approximately 3.61 acres and is positioned adjacent to the Skudai Highway with access to Jalan Tun Abdul Razak. Nearby landmarks include Istana Bukit Serene (1.3 km), Beletime Danga Bay (2.4 km), KPJ Puteri Specialist Hospital (5.3 km), and the Bukit Chagar RTS Link Station (7.7 km).

Connectivity & Transport

Is there a free shuttle service from Calia Residences?

Yes. A complimentary shuttle service is planned to connect residents to the Sultan Iskandar CIQ Complex and the Bukit Chagar RTS Link Station, both approximately 7.7 km from the development. Shuttle schedules, frequency and routes should be confirmed with the developer's sales team. This service is proposed and may be subject to change.

How far is Calia Residences from the RTS Link?

The Bukit Chagar RTS Link Station is approximately 7.7 km from Calia Residences. A free shuttle service is planned to cover this journey. The JB–Singapore RTS Link is targeted for full operations from early 2027, connecting Bukit Chagar (JB) to Woodlands North (Singapore) in approximately 5 minutes. Confirm the current RTS status independently before making any purchase decision based on RTS proximity.

What are the main road connections?

Calia Residences sits adjacent to the Skudai Highway and is linked to Jalan Tun Abdul Razak — providing straightforward access to JB City Centre, Larkin Sentral, and key expressway links to the rest of Johor and beyond.

Units & Facilities

What are the unit types at Calia Residences?

Calia Residences has 4 unit types across 2 towers (1,274 units total, 22 per floor, 5 lifts per tower): Type A — 453 sqft, 1 bed 1 bath, 290 total units; Type B — 656 sqft, 1+1 bed 1 bath, 288 total units; Type C — 771 sqft, 2 bed 2 bath, 522 total units; Type D — 984 sqft, 3 bed 3 bath (dual-key), 174 total units. Types A–C come with 1 parking lot; Type D comes with 2 parking lots. All units include a private balcony and functional yard.

What is included in the partial furnishing?

All units at Calia Residences are partially furnished with 6 standard items: 5-star rated air conditioners (in the living area and all bedrooms); 5-star rated ceiling fans (in the living area and master bedroom); digital lock (front door); kitchen cabinet; kitchen hood and hob; wardrobes in all bedrooms. These items are per the developer's brochure (Oct 2025) and are subject to confirmation in the SPA.

What facilities does Calia Residences have?

Calia Residences has 28 holistic lifestyle facilities on Level 7 and 7A (Mezzanine). Community Hub: Co-Working Space, Party Terrace, Event Terrace, Premium Lounge, Entertainment Room. Workout & Energetic Living: Pickleball Court, Fitness Centre, Yoga Room, Lap Pool, Outdoor Fitness. Restful Nook: Jacuzzi, Floating Cabana, Shallow Pool, Cascading Pool, Poolside Terrace, Garden Terrace, Herbs Garden, Indoor Games Room. Family-Oriented: Family Terrace, BBQ Area, Multipurpose Room, Gourmet Kitchen, Private Lounge. Kids' Corner: Kids Fun Pool, Kids Adventure Zone, Kids Discovery Room. Other: Party Lounge, Surau.

What sustainability features are included?

Calia Residences holds a GreenRE Gold Certificate for eco-conscious development. Features include EV charging stations throughout the development and a 5-star energy-rated air conditioning and ceiling fan package in all units. GreenRE Gold covers energy efficiency, water conservation, indoor environment quality and sustainable site practices.
