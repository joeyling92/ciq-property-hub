FAQ

Questions answered.

How far is Gen Sphere from the CIQ checkpoint?
Gen Sphere is approximately 480m from the JB CIQ checkpoint — about a 6–7 minute walk. JB Sentral (the main bus and KTM hub for cross-border travel) is even closer at 440m. For Singapore commuters, this is one of the most walkable locations in JB City Centre.
What is the RTS Bukit Chagar and why does it matter?
The Johor Bahru–Singapore RTS Link (Rapid Transit System) is a cross-border rail link currently under construction. The Bukit Chagar station — the JB terminus — is approximately 700m from Gen Sphere. When operational, it will allow direct, fast travel between JB and Woodlands North in Singapore, dramatically improving cross-border commuter convenience.
What unit types are available and what are the sizes?
Gen Sphere offers 1 Bedroom, 2 Bedrooms, 3 Bedrooms, and Duplex configurations. Built-up sizes range from 459 sq ft to 755 sq ft as stated in the developer's APDL disclosure. Pricing runs from RM609,000 to RM1,120,000. Register interest below for exact unit-by-unit sizes, floor levels, and current availability.
Is there a Bumiputera discount?
Yes — the developer offers a 15% Bumiputera discount on eligible units. Register interest below and confirm eligibility upon follow-up.
When is the expected completion date?
The expected date of completion is December 2029, based on the developer's APDL disclosure. All completion dates are subject to the Sale & Purchase Agreement between buyer and developer.
Why is Gen Sphere a good investment?
Gen Sphere benefits from three converging factors: (1) an ultra-walkable location — 480m to CIQ and 700m to the future RTS — that directly serves the growing JB–Singapore commuter market; (2) freehold tenure in JB City Centre, which is rare and underpins long-term capital value; and (3) an entry price from RM609,000 that positions it as one of the more accessible freehold city centre options available. Rental demand in this corridor is consistently driven by Singapore-based workers and JB commuters.
