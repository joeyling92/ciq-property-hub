FAQ

Questions answered
plainly.

Is Paragon Gateway freehold or leasehold?
Paragon Gateway holds a freehold title — permanent land ownership with no expiry date. This is a meaningful distinction in Johor's property market, where freehold titles are increasingly rare in newer developments and command long-term value retention.
What does "partially furnished" mean?
Paragon Gateway units are delivered with key furniture and fittings included in the purchase price. This reduces the cost and delay between vacant possession and being move-in or rental-ready. The exact specification is confirmed in the Sale & Purchase Agreement — register interest below for the current furnishing schedule.
What is the free shuttle bus service?
A complimentary shuttle bus is provided for residents to the CIQ (Customs, Immigration and Quarantine) checkpoint, supporting Singapore commuters and cross-border travellers. Operating schedules are managed by building management — register interest below for current times.
Can foreigners buy Paragon Gateway?
Yes, subject to the minimum foreign purchase price threshold set by the Johor state government. Register interest below for current eligibility requirements and financing options for foreign buyers.
What facilities are at the Level 10 Recreation Deck?
The Level 10 Recreation Deck includes 32+ amenities: 50m lap pool, jacuzzi, splash pool, sauna & steam rooms, gymnasium, dance studio, squash court, kids' play area, function rooms, lawn & garden, and lounge deck. All residents across four towers have equal access.
What unit types and sizes are available?
Five unit configurations: Type A (1 Bedroom 1 Bathroom, 499 sq ft), Type B (1 Bedroom 1 Bathroom, 648 sq ft), Type C (2 Bedrooms 2 Bathrooms, 788 sq ft), Type D (3 Bedrooms 2 Bathrooms, 915 sq ft), and Type E (3 Bedrooms 3 Bathrooms, 1,177 sq ft). Types A, B and C are available for 3D virtual tour via Matterport. Current availability and pricing upon enquiry — register interest below.
