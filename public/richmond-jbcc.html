08 — FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Project & Developer

What is Richmond JBCC?

Richmond JBCC is a freehold hotel-suites development by Richmond Asia Group in the Johor Bahru Central Business District, professionally managed under the Hyatt Place brand of Hyatt Hotels Corporation. It sits beside Tan Hiok Nee Heritage Street, inside the Iskandar Special Economic Zone (Iskandar SEZ) and the Ibrahim International Business District (IIBD), with completion expected Q2 2028.

Who is the developer and who manages the hotel?

The developer is Richmond Asia Group. Hotel management is under the Hyatt Place brand — a brand of Hyatt Hotels Corporation. These are two separate entities. Richmond Asia also developed Richmond Mayor, a Capri by Fraser hotel-suite project in Mount Austin JB. Richmond Estelar in Kuala Lumpur is where the complimentary owner nights (years 1–5) are offered.

When is Richmond JBCC expected to complete?

Expected completion is Q2 2028. This is a target timeline and is subject to change. The Sale & Purchase Agreement governs actual completion obligations.

Location & Singapore Connectivity

How close is Richmond JBCC to Singapore?

Richmond JBCC is 1.6 km from Bukit Chagar RTS station. The JB–Singapore Rapid Transit System (RTS) Link is a ~4 km cross-border rail journey between Bukit Chagar (JB) and Woodlands North (Singapore), with the crossing taking approximately 5 minutes. Passenger service is expected by end-2026, with full operations anticipated from early 2027. Confirm current RTS status independently as timelines are subject to change.

What is near Richmond JBCC in JB?

Tan Hiok Nee Heritage Street is 50 m away. JB City Square is 650 m, R&F Marina Shopping Mall 750 m, Komtar JBCC 900 m, Sultan Iskandar CIQ Complex 1.0 km, JB Sentral 1.1 km. The project is inside the Iskandar Special Economic Zone and the Ibrahim International Business District, placing it at the centre of JB's commercial and heritage corridor.

Investment & Rental Returns

What is the Richmond JBCC Guaranteed Rental Return (GRR)?

Published marketing materials outline a 15-year programme: Years 1–3 at 6% p.a. guaranteed rental return on purchase price; Years 4–5 at 7% p.a. (32% total over 5 years). From Year 6–15, net profit sharing of 70% to owners and 30% to the operator, with an option to renew for another 10 years. A developer buyback option exists after Year 10 at approximately 3% p.a. premium. All terms are subject to the official SPA and GRR contract — read the full agreement and seek independent advice before committing.

Are there other ownership benefits?

Yes. Published benefits include: 5 complimentary nights per year at Richmond Estelar, Kuala Lumpur during Years 1–5; fully furnished hotel-grade fit-out included; hands-off management under Hyatt Place; and the developer buyback option after Year 10. Confirm all benefits in the official sales documentation.

What does Hyatt Place management mean for owners?

Suites are operated under the Hyatt Place brand of Hyatt Hotels Corporation — fully furnished and professionally managed, with owners participating in the rental income programme without self-managing. Day-to-day hotel operations, guest bookings and room management are handled by the hotel operator. Owner personal use is subject to hotel availability and the applicable programme terms.

Eligibility & Purchase

Can Singaporeans and foreigners buy Richmond JBCC?

Yes. Richmond JBCC is open to foreign buyers, including Singaporeans, subject to Johor's minimum purchase price threshold for foreigners. Buyers should confirm current eligibility requirements, applicable state levy or foreign buyer duties, and any additional approvals with a qualified Malaysian solicitor before proceeding.

What are the unit types and sizes?

Richmond JBCC offers two unit types: Type A — Hotel Rooms in Twin and King bed configurations at 301 sqft (from RM 1,020,000); and Type C — Suites with a separate Living and Dining area at up to 614 sqft (pricing upon enquiry). All units are fully furnished to hotel grade.

Can I view Richmond JBCC online or in person?

A 360° virtual tour is available on the developer's website. To arrange an in-person gallery visit, register interest below for viewing appointments and the latest price list and floor plans.

What financing is available for a hotel-suite purchase?

Hotel suites carry a commercial title, which differs from residential strata properties. Financing options, loan-to-value ratios and bank eligibility for commercial-titled hotel suites may differ from residential property financing. Check with your preferred bank early in the process, and consult an independent solicitor on the applicable stamp duties, RPGT, and foreign buyer levies.
