08 — FAQ & GALLERY

Frequently Asked Questions

Project & Location

What is The Iconic by PGB?

The Iconic by PGB is a freehold serviced apartment development at Jalan Indera Putera, Stulang Darat, Johor Bahru. The project comprises twin towers with a total of 1,510 units, positioned approximately 2 km from the Bukit Chagar RTS and the Sultan Iskandar CIQ complex. It is developed by PGB Iconic Sdn Bhd, a wholly-owned subsidiary of Paragon Globe Berhad (listed on Bursa Malaysia's Main Market).

Where exactly is The Iconic located?

The Iconic is located at Jalan Indera Putera, Stulang Darat, Johor Bahru, Johor. Key nearby destinations include JB Sentral (1.8 km), Bukit Chagar RTS (2.0 km), CIQ Sultan Iskandar (2.0 km), R&F Mall (1.4 km), Open University Malaysia (600 m) and Foon Yew High School (1.3 km). The development has highway access via the EDL and Tebrau Highway corridors.

Is The Iconic freehold and under the Housing Development Act?

Yes. The Iconic is a freehold development. It is registered as serviced apartments under the Housing Development Act (HDA) with a commercial title and no retail units within the building.

Who is the developer of The Iconic by PGB?

The developer is PGB Iconic Sdn Bhd, a wholly-owned subsidiary of Paragon Globe Berhad (listed on the Main Market of Bursa Malaysia). Joland Group is a related but separate private entity and is not the developer of The Iconic by PGB.

Towers & Units

How many towers and units are planned?

The Iconic comprises two towers on approximately 2.7 acres. Tower A has 719 units and is the current detailed launch. Tower B (proposed) has 791 units — its pricing, unit breakdown and launch details are to be confirmed separately.

What Tower A unit types and sizes are available?

Tower A offers three unit types: Type A (526 sqft, 1 bedroom, 1 bathroom), Type B (756 sqft, 2 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms), and Type C (956 sqft, 3 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms — dual-key layout). Each residential floor has 20 units. Residential levels run from Level 12 to Level 47.

What are the proposed Tower A prices?

Type A (526 sqft) is proposed at RM 578,600 to RM 619,600. Pricing for Types B and C is available upon enquiry with the sales team. All prices are proposed and subject to change. Please confirm current pricing directly with the project sales team.

How many parking spaces come with a Tower A unit?

Type A units come with 1 residential parking space. Parking allocation for Types B and C should be confirmed with the sales team. Residential parking is allocated across Levels 1–10, with visitor parking at LG1 and motorcycle parking at LG2.

Facilities & Operations

How many facilities does The Iconic provide?

The Iconic is planned with 52 multigenerational facilities across approximately 1.85 acres: 42 at Level 11 and 10 on the rooftop level. Facilities include pools, fitness areas, children's zones, co-working spaces, social lounges, padel, and four rooftop pickleball courts.

Is a complimentary shuttle service planned?

Yes. A complimentary shuttle service is proposed connecting The Iconic with JB Sentral and R&F stops. Morning peak (5:30am–9:30am) runs every 20–25 minutes. Evening peak (4:30pm–8:30pm) runs every 20 minutes. Off-peak and late-night services also run with lower frequency. The schedule is indicative and subject to change without notice.

What services does Aurum Stay provide?

Aurum Stay is a proposed one-stop homestay management and resident-service provider. Services include 24-hour front desk, professional housekeeping, interior design consultation, booking-platform support, private transport (by advance booking), and handyman services. All services are subject to Aurum Stay's terms and availability.

What is the estimated maintenance fee?

The estimated maintenance fee is RM 0.38 per sqft per month. This is an estimate only and subject to confirmation in the management documentation and the Sale & Purchase Agreement.

Specifications & Eligibility

What partial furnishing is planned for Tower A?

All Tower A unit types are planned with: 5-star-rated air conditioners (Daikin or equivalent, via voucher) for living area and all bedrooms; 5-star-rated ceiling fans (KDK or equivalent) for living area and master bedroom; a digital lock (Sharp or equivalent); a fitted kitchen cabinet; and a kitchen hood and hob (Electrolux or equivalent). Brands are indicative and subject to change.

What security and green features are planned?

4-tier security: guard house → car park access → lift and lobby control → digital lock at unit. Green features include GreenRE Gold certification, rainwater harvesting, EV charging readiness, and low-VOC paint. Continuous accessibility ramps are planned across the multigenerational facility areas.

Can foreigners buy a unit at The Iconic?

Yes. Foreign buyers are eligible for units priced RM 500,000 and above. Confirm current eligibility requirements and any applicable state levy obligations with the sales team, as terms may have changed.

Purchase Guidance

When is The Iconic expected to be completed?

Completion is targeted within 60 months from the date the Sale & Purchase Agreement is signed. No fixed calendar date has been published. This is a target and the Sale & Purchase Agreement governs actual completion obligations.
