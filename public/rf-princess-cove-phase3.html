The Investment Thesis

Southeast Asia's Shenzhen–Hong Kong moment is here.

When Shenzhen opened its borders to Hong Kong, property at the crossing became some of the most valuable in Asia. Johor Bahru and Singapore are living that same story — and R&F Princess Cove sits at the exact crossing point.

At just 650 metres from Woodlands Checkpoint, New Casa Suites is not merely close to Singapore — it is functionally part of the Singapore economic orbit, at Malaysian land prices. The RTS Link, now 83% complete, will shrink a border crossing to a 5-minute ride.

The result: a property that earns Singapore-adjacent rental premiums while being priced as Johor real estate. Malaysia's most-viewed condominium and rental project for a reason.

FAQ

Common questions,
answered plainly.

How far is New Casa Suites from Singapore?
The development is approximately 650 metres from the Woodlands Checkpoint (CIQ) border crossing. A link bridge connects directly from Phase 3 to the CIQ building. Once the RTS Link opens, residents will have direct rail access to Woodlands RTS Station in Singapore in approximately 5 minutes, reaching the CBD in 35–40 minutes total.
Can foreigners buy this property?
Yes. R&F Princess Cove New Casa Suites is open to foreign buyers. The Phase 3 sale package includes a waiver of the Foreigner Consent Fee. Contact Terry for current foreign buyer eligibility, minimum price thresholds, and financing options.
What is the RTS Link and when will it open?
The Rapid Transit System (RTS) Link is a cross-border light rail connection between JB Sentral (Malaysia) and Woodlands North Station (Singapore). As of the Phase 3 briefing, the project is 83% complete with operations targeted for 2026. The Bukit Chagar RTS station is adjacent to the R&F Princess Cove masterplan.
What rental can I expect from this property?
Based on 2026 market data from R&F Princess Cove listings: Studios rent for RM 2,300–2,700/mo; 1BR for RM 2,300–2,800/mo; 2BR for RM 3,000–3,500/mo; 3BR for RM 4,000–5,000/mo; and 4BR for RM 5,000–6,000/mo. These are market reference rates based on current listings and actual income may vary by unit, floor, and conditions.
What does the Phase 3 free package actually include?
The developer absorbs: (1) legal fees on the SPA, (2) legal fees on the Loan Agreement, (3) stamp duty on the Loan Agreement, (4) Memorandum on Transfer fees, (5) Foreigner Consent Fee, and (6) fixed car park allocation. Confirm current terms with Terry as these packages can be updated.
Is there a link between Phase 3 and R&F Mall?
Yes. A dedicated link bridge connects Phase 3 (New Casa Suites) directly to R&F Mall, allowing residents to access retail, F&B, and entertainment without going outdoors. This is an exclusive Phase 3 amenity in addition to the Level 4 and Level 6 facility floors.
