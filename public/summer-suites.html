FAQ

Questions answered.

How far is Summer Suites from the JB CIQ checkpoint?
Summer Suites is approximately 850m from the JB CIQ checkpoint — around a 10–11 minute walk. The project is situated just off Jalan Bukit Meldrum in JB City Centre, well within walkable range of the main Singapore-bound crossing.
What is the Bukit Chagar RTS Station and why does it matter?
The Johor Bahru–Singapore RTS Link is a cross-border rapid transit system currently under construction. The Bukit Chagar station — the JB terminus — is approximately 850m from Summer Suites. When operational, it will allow direct rail travel to Woodlands North MRT in Singapore in under 10 minutes, dramatically upgrading the commuter experience from this address.
What unit types are available?
Summer Suites offers three configurations: Type A (3 bedrooms, 3 bathrooms, 912 sq ft), Type B (2+1 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, 808 sq ft), and Type C (Dual-Key Studio + 2 bathrooms, 599 sq ft). Pricing from RM620,000. Register interest below for the current price list and available units.
What is a dual-key unit and how does it work?
A dual-key unit (Type C at 599 sq ft) contains two separate lockable sections under one land title. You can live in one side and rent the other, rent both sides independently, or use one for Airbnb while keeping the other for personal use. It maximises yield on a single freehold title — particularly attractive in a high-commuter-traffic corridor like JB CIQ.
What facilities does Summer Suites offer?
Over 20 facilities on Level 10, including: Half Olympic Swimming Pool, Jacuzzi, Gym, Outdoor Gym, Sauna, Co-Working Space, Media Room, Social Lounge, 24-Hour AI Mart, BBQ Area, Children's Pool, Children's Playground, Multi-Purpose Hall, Outdoor Terrace, Sunken Lounge, Chill Corner, Hoop Station, WOW Room, The Soap Bar (Laundromat), and Learning Center.
Is Summer Suites a good investment?
Summer Suites' investment case rests on three pillars: (1) Location — 850m to CIQ and the future RTS Station places it squarely in the path of Singapore commuter demand; (2) Freehold tenure — rare in JB City Centre, underpinning long-term capital value; and (3) Dual-key flexibility — Type C allows two rental income streams from one title. The cross-border commuter corridor consistently drives rental demand, and RTS connectivity when operational is expected to accelerate it further.
