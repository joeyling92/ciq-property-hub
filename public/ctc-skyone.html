Unit Concepts

Flexible layouts built
for modern living.

CTC SKYONE fully adopts Dual-Key and Triple-Key design concepts — offering 2BR to 4BR configurations designed for ownstay, rental yield, or multi-generational living.

Dual-Key
Two access points. One investment.
A dual-key unit operates as two separate living spaces within a single strata title — typically a primary suite and a studio, each with its own entrance.
  • Live in one, rent out the other
  • Maximise rental yield from one unit
  • Privacy for extended family living
  • Ideal for mixed ownstay + investment
Triple-Key
Three streams. Maximum flexibility.
A triple-key configuration unlocks three independent living zones within one title — offering the highest rental diversification and multi-use potential available.
  • Three separate income streams possible
  • Short-term + long-term rental mix
  • Multi-generational family accommodation
  • Superior investment yield potential

FAQ

Your questions answered.

How far is CTC SKYONE from the RTS Bukit Chagar Station?
CTC SKYONE is approximately 300 metres from the proposed RTS Bukit Chagar Station — making it one of the closest freehold developments to the RTS Link in JB City Centre. The station connects directly to Woodlands North in Singapore via the Johor Bahru–Singapore RTS Link. From the doorstep, it's a comfortable 5-minute walk.
What is a Dual-Key or Triple-Key unit?
A Dual-Key unit has two independent living zones within a single strata title — each with its own entrance. This lets you live in one zone and rent out the other, house extended family separately, or run mixed short-term and long-term rentals. A Triple-Key unit extends this to three independent zones — maximising rental income diversification within one property title.
Is the tenure freehold? What does Commercial Strata mean?
Yes — CTC SKYONE is Freehold in tenure, which is rare for JB City Centre properties. The title is Commercial Strata, meaning each unit is individually strata-titled under the Strata Titles Act. Although the development type is Serviced Apartment, it falls under the Housing Development Act (HDA), providing buyers with the usual HDA protections including the standard Sale & Purchase Agreement.
What unit sizes and configurations are available?
CTC SKYONE offers a range of 2-bedroom to 4-bedroom layouts, including dual-key and triple-key configurations. Exact unit sizes are upon enquiry — register interest below for a full breakdown of current available units, floor levels, orientations, and pricing. The Efficient Modern Layouts concept is applied throughout, ensuring optimal usable area and natural lighting in every configuration.
Which tower should I buy in?
Tower A (57 storeys) is the tallest and offers the highest floor options with the widest panoramic views. Towers B and C (56 storeys each) offer different orientations and unit mix options. The right tower depends on your preferred view, floor level, unit layout, and budget. Register interest below for a personalised comparison of currently available units across all three towers.
What is the investment case for CTC SKYONE?
CTC SKYONE sits at the intersection of three powerful investment drivers: (1) proximity to the RTS Link which will bring Singapore-Malaysia cross-border commuting to walking distance, (2) freehold tenure in Zone A Iskandar — a rare combination that underpins long-term capital value, and (3) dual/triple-key flexibility that supports multiple rental income streams from a single unit. These factors combined make it one of the stronger rental yield and capital appreciation plays in JB City Centre.
