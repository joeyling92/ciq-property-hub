FAQ

Questions, answered.

What unit types are available at The Address JB?
Three unit types: Type A — The Studio (450 sq ft, 1BR+1BA), Type B — The Edge (660 sq ft, 2BR+2BA, Variants B1 & B2), and Type C — The Residence (865 sq ft, 3BR+2BA, Dual-Key concept). 3D virtual tours are available for Type A and Type B via Lapentor.
What does GreenRE certified mean for residents?
GreenRE is Malaysia's green building certification — it means The Address was designed and built to meet standards for energy efficiency, water conservation, indoor air quality, and sustainable materials. In practice this translates to lower utility bills, better air quality, and a reduced environmental footprint compared to conventional high-rises.
How close is The Address to Singapore?
The Address is approximately 3km from the CIQ checkpoint and 3.2km from the JB-Singapore Causeway. JB Sentral (the main transit hub for cross-border buses and KTM) is 4km away. The future RTS Link will further improve cross-border connectivity.
What amenities are at The Address JB?
All 40+ amenities are located on the Level 11 Facilities Deck: 25m Infinity Pool, Water Cocoon, Aqua Gym, Pool Deck, Children's Pool, Sunken Lounger, Multipurpose Lawn, Terraced Seating, Basketball Court, Pickleball Court, Garden Lawn, Pergola Seating, Gymnasium, Outdoor Gym, Open Studio, Games Room, Sauna & Locker, Study Lounge, Working Area, Social Kitchen, Residence Lounge, Multipurpose Hall, Laundry, Surau, BBQ Area, Forest Garden, Jogging Path, Children's Playground, Fitness Station, Quiet Garden, Terrace, and Outdoor Lounge.
What is the Dual-Key concept in Type C?
The Dual-Key concept in Type C (The Residence) allows the unit to function as two separate living spaces — typically a main home and a smaller studio or bedroom with its own entrance. This enables flexible uses: live in one and rent out the other, or house extended family with privacy. Confirm the exact specification via the Register Interest form below.
Can I do a virtual tour before deciding?
Yes — The Address provides fully navigable 3D virtual tours for Type A (Studio) and Type B (The Edge) via Lapentor. You can explore the space from any device, at any time. Type C does not currently have a virtual tour — register interest below to arrange a physical showroom visit.
